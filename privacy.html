Privacy Policy

This is a personal portfolio site. It is intentionally built to collect as little data about visitors as possible — there is no analytics script and no cookies by default.

What this site does NOT do

  • No analytics or tracking scripts are loaded by default.
  • No cookies are set by default.
  • No visitor data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No third-party advertising is served.

The contact form

If you use the contact form, the information you type (name, email, and message) is used only to open a pre-filled email in your own email client — it is not stored on this site or any server operated by this site.

Fonts

This site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Loading a font from Google's servers may expose your IP address to Google as part of that request, in line with Google's privacy policy. No other data is shared with Google by this site.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site evolves. Check back periodically for changes.
